MODEL INFORMATION

Engineering source fidelity

This application is a standalone, dependency-free WebGL2 engineering reconstruction of the BADR-1 flare system. It includes the 10 drawing-referenced piping systems, vessel, pump, ignition rack, stack, support representations, diagnostic annotations and an issue register.

Important limitation: Lengths in material bills are not automatically actual centerline route lengths. The site coordinates, some nozzle orientations, rack internals, support layout and proprietary flare tip geometry are provisional. Never use this model for fabrication, field isolation or operation.

Safety: No operating pressures, interlocks, ignition timers or startup instructions in this software constitute approved BADR-1 settings. The ZEECO manuals included in source records refer to general technology and another sales order.
